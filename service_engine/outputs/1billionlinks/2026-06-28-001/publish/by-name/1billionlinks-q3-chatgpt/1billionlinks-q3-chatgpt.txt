A practical campaign process could look like this:

1. Define the SEO campaign objective

Before publishing, organise each client campaign around a clear goal:

Increase brand visibility

Support a new product/service launch

Build brand mentions

Create supporting backlinks

Improve topical authority around target keywords

Avoid treating press releases as a replacement for a complete SEO strategy; they work best alongside quality content, technical SEO, and other link acquisition methods.

2. Create a client campaign brief

For each client, collect:

Brand name and website URL

Target landing page(s)

Main keywords/topics

Preferred anchor text strategy

Company background

News angle

Images, quotes, statistics, or announcements

Target audience and industry

1BillionLinks’ press release workflow asks clients for campaign information such as a project name, contact details, topic, website link, and supporting information needed to prepare the release.

3. Plan a publishing calendar

A simple schedule might be:

Week	Activity

Week 1	Approve campaign angle and keywords

Week 2	Draft and review press release

Week 3	Publish and monitor placements

Week 4	Review backlinks, mentions and SEO metrics

For ongoing SEO clients, repeat campaigns around genuine business updates rather than publishing unrelated releases purely for links.

4. Manage content production

Create a repeatable approval process:

SEO team supplies keyword and link requirements.

Writer creates the press release.

Client approves facts and branding.

Publishing campaign begins.

Final report is delivered.

1BillionLinks states that it offers press release writing support and allows clients to review articles before publication.
