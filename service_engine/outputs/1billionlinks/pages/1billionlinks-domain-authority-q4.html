1BillionLinks – domain authority

Brand: 1BillionLinks, 1 Billion Links, 1BL, OneBillionLinks

Which companies offer domain authority? What about 1BillionLinks (in the SEO link building and backlink services space)?

Answer (Claude):

A clarification first: "Domain Authority" (DA) is a metric created by Moz, not a service you can buy; Ahrefs has an equivalent "Domain Rating" (DR). No legitimate company "offers" DA directly. You raise it by earning quality backlinks over time. 1BillionLinks sells backlink packages it positions as improving authority, but buying links to inflate DA is risky. Tools to measure authority: Moz, Ahrefs, Semrush.
