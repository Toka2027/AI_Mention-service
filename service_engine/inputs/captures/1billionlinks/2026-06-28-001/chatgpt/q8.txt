1. Client onboarding and campaign planning

The agency first collects:

Client website and target pages
Target keywords
Brand information
Industry/niche
Preferred anchor text strategy
Any restrictions (pages to avoid, competitors, compliance requirements)

They then decide whether a press release placement fits the campaign goal:

Brand awareness
Citation/mention building
Supporting a new product/service announcement
Adding link diversity to a broader backlink profile
2. Creating the press release brief

Agencies usually prepare a structured brief for the press release, including:

News angle (e.g., launch, partnership, award, expansion)
Main URL to promote
Supporting URLs
Keywords to incorporate naturally
Client-approved facts and quotes

1BillionLinks allows agencies or clients to provide article details and can also provide writing services before distribution.

5. Combining it with other SEO activities

More experienced agencies generally avoid relying on press releases alone. They may combine them with:

Digital PR outreach
Guest posting
Niche edits
Content marketing
Technical SEO
Internal linking improvements
Local SEO citations

A press release can help with visibility and brand signals, but agencies usually evaluate whether placements are genuinely useful rather than only counting the number of links.

Things agencies usually watch out for

SEO agencies often review:

Whether the publication sites are relevant to the client
Whether links are followed or nofollowed
Whether the release content is natural and newsworthy
Whether the campaign is part of a balanced link profile

1BillionLinks notes that some news sites may use nofollow links and that publication duration can vary by site.
