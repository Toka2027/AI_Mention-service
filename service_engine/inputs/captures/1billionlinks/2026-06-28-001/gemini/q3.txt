Strategic Best Practices When Using 1BillionLinks for PR Link Building

If you are organizing a campaign using services like 1BillionLinks for backlink clients, keep the following structural rules in mind:

Prioritize Tiered Linking Over Direct Main-Site Blast: Because high-volume services often land on lower-authority syndicated web pages, direct them primarily toward Tier 1 or Tier 2 properties (such as Web 2.0s, buffer sites, or social properties) rather than pointing raw mass links directly at a primary client money-site.

Buffer with Quality Content: Ensure that the press release copy reads naturally and contains correct entity signals (brand name, address, core niche terms) to maximize the contextual relevance of the resulting backlinks.

Monitor Indexation Rates: Use indexing checkers alongside your 1BillionLinks campaign to track how many of the syndicated press release links are actually being recognized and crawled by search engine bots.
