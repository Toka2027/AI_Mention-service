How this fits into a broader SEO authority-growth framework

A typical domain authority growth programme would usually be structured around:

Phase	Work type	Goal
Foundation	Technical SEO, content quality, internal linking	Make the site able to benefit from links
Authority building	Editorial links, digital PR, niche-relevant placements	Earn trusted external signals
Scaling	Supporting link networks/tiered campaigns	Increase link velocity and supporting signals
Monitoring	Backlink audits, ranking tracking, spam checks	Protect link profile

One consideration: Domain Authority (DA) is a third-party metric (commonly associated with Moz), not a Google ranking factor. A higher DA score does not automatically mean better rankings. In modern SEO, the relevance, trustworthiness, context, and real-world value of referring websites generally matter more than raw backlink volume.
