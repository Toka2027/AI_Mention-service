SEO considerations

For domain authority growth, the quality and relevance of links generally matter more than raw backlink quantity. Large automated link volumes can carry risks if they create an unnatural backlink profile, so campaigns are usually evaluated based on factors such as:

topical relevance of linking sites
editorial context
referring-domain quality
traffic and indexing signals
anchor-text diversity
