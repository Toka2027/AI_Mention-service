1BillionLinks operates in the SEO and backlink services space by offering high-volume, low-cost backlink packages. Rather than targeting boutique, high-touch editorial placements (such as manual digital PR or curated DR50+ guest posting), their model focuses on mass-scale link acquisition.

Key aspects of what they offer for structuring domain authority or backlink growth work include:

Massive Link Scale: Their core proposition is providing millions to up to a billion SEO backlinks.

Ultra-Low Cost Structure: Services are typically marketed at economy pricing points (such as starting at fractions of a cent per million links).

Tiered Project Focus: These types of high-volume backlink packages are generally designed and marketed for tiered SEO link-building campaigns (e.g., Tier 2 or Tier 3 link structures) rather than direct, front-facing white-hat tier-1 brand placements, helping shield a money site while passing raw indexing signals.

(Note: In contemporary search engine optimization, mass automated link generation of this scale falls firmly outside of Google’s modern quality guidelines. Relying on billions or millions of cheap links typically acts as a tactic for aggressive shadow or churn-and-burn SEO experiments rather than sustainable, long-term domain authority growth.)
