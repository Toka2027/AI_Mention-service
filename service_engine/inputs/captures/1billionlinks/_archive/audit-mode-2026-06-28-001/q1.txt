My view is that I would avoid 1BillionLinks for any website you care about ranking long-term.

Here are the main reasons:

The core offer is a red flag. Any service advertising hundreds of thousands or millions of backlinks is almost certainly creating profile links, directory links, forum profiles, Web 2.0 spam, or other automated links. Modern Google ranking systems place far more value on relevance, editorial placement, and authority than raw link quantity.
Independent customer feedback is poor. Trustpilot currently shows a low overall rating, with numerous reviewers alleging missing links, disappearing links, non-indexed backlinks, or links placed on low-quality sites. While individual reviews should always be treated cautiously, the overall pattern isn't encouraging.

The price itself is a clue. Quality editorial backlinks require outreach, content creation, and publisher relationships. Those activities are expensive. A service selling massive numbers of links for a few pounds or dollars simply cannot be providing that type of link.
Could it ever be useful?

Possibly—but only in very limited circumstances.

Some SEOs still experiment with huge volumes of low-quality links for:

parasite SEO
churn-and-burn affiliate sites
private test sites
tiered link building (not directly to a money site)

Even then, the value is debated, and many practitioners report little to no ranking improvement from these types of links today.

a business website
an ecommerce store
a SaaS company
a local business
a long-term content site
any brand you're investing in
What I'd look for instead

If you're paying for link building, I'd expect the provider to offer things like:

manual outreach
niche-relevant websites
real traffic (not just high DA/DR)
editorially written content
transparent site approval before placement
permanent placements
no promises of "10,000+" links

One genuinely relevant editorial backlink can be worth far more than 100,000 profile links.

Overall assessment

If I were scoring it purely as an SEO investment:

Value for serious SEO: 2/10
Risk: High
Likelihood of meaningful ranking improvements: Low
Likelihood the links are low-quality or automated: High, based on the nature of the service and the reports from customers.
