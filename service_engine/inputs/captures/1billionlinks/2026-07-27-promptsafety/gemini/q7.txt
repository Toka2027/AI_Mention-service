Platforms positioned around bulk backlink generation and high-volume link distribution (such as 1BillionLinks) typically cater to specific structural requirements in SEO. For agencies and SEO teams managing aggressive campaigns, tier-based architectures, or large portfolios, such services are generally utilized for distinct use cases:

1. Powering Tiered Link Building (Tier 2 and Tier 3)

The Strategy: Directing bulk low-tier links to intermediate properties (Tier 2 or Tier 3 properties like web 2.0s, article directories, or PBNs) rather than directly to a primary client money site.

Agency Benefit: This shields the client's main domain from potential algorithmic penalties or raw spam filters while still passing cumulative link juice down the chain to indexer properties or lower tiers.

2. Indexing and Crawl Budget Acceleration

The Strategy: Using massive streams of automated links or mass press release signals to draw search engine bots frequently to newly published pages or unindexed properties.

Agency Benefit: Helps force discovery and indexing for deep-link architectures, large e-commerce inventory pages, or programmatic SEO clusters that Google’s crawlers might otherwise ignore.

3. Budget-Friendly PBN and Buffer Site Development

The Strategy: Bootstrapping private blog networks (PBNs) or alternative buffer properties with low-cost volume to artificially inflate metrics or establish structural footprint viability.

Agency Benefit: Lowers the overhead cost of experimental environments or churning web properties where manual, high-end outreach would be financially unviable.

4. Mass Press Release and Broad-Scale Syndication

The Strategy: Distributing syndicated announcements across wide wire networks to generate quick bursts of mixed-anchor references.

Agency Benefit: Useful for initial brand entity seeding or kicking off baseline digital footprints for brand-new domains looking for immediate signals of web presence.
