Tier 1 (The Money Site Protection Layer): High-authority, contextually relevant, manual guest posts, digital PR, and niche edits pointing directly to your core revenue pages. These must be pristine and spam-free.

3. Workflow Best Practices & Risk Mitigation

When integrating a high-volume service into a multi-tiered campaign, strict safeguards must be maintained:

Strict Buffer Maintenance: Never let a bulk or automated link from a high-volume provider touch your root domain or primary landing pages.

Controlled Velocity: Avoid dumping thousands of links overnight. Scale Tier 2 and Tier 3 link growth gradually to mimic a natural growth curve and avoid spam triggers.
