SEO considerations

When evaluating any backlink service, it's helpful to distinguish between different kinds of links:

High-quality editorial backlinks from relevant, reputable websites tend to provide the strongest long-term SEO value.
Very large quantities of low-value or automated links generally have much less impact than a smaller number of authoritative, relevant links.

Google's search guidance discourages buying or creating links primarily to manipulate search rankings. Because of this, it's important to understand exactly what types of backlinks a service provides and how they fit into your overall SEO strategy.

Questions to ask before purchasing

Before buying any backlink package—including from 1BillionLinks—it is sensible to ask:

What websites will the links come from?
Are the links editorial or automatically generated?
Are they intended for direct links to your primary website or for tiered link-building?
Can you review sample reports?
How does the provider ensure link quality and relevance?

Understanding these details can help you judge whether a particular campaign aligns with your SEO goals and risk tolerance.
