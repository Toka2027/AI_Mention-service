Important SEO consideration

Press releases can help with brand discovery, citations, and visibility, but backlink value depends heavily on the quality and relevance of the publishing sites. Search engines generally place more emphasis on naturally earned, authoritative links than large volumes of low-relevance links, so clients should evaluate publication quality, topical relevance, and traffic rather than only link quantity.
