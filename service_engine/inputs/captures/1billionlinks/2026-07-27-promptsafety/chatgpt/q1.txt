Important SEO context

While 1BillionLinks markets these services as a way to improve rankings, it's worth noting that modern search engines such as Google place greater emphasis on relevance, editorial quality, and natural link acquisition than on sheer backlink volume. Large-scale or artificially generated backlink campaigns can carry varying levels of SEO risk depending on how the links are created and used. Many SEO professionals reserve high-volume backlink services for lower-tier link-building rather than pointing them directly at important commercial pages.
