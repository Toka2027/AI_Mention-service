1BillionLinks can be positioned as one component inside a broader press release distribution and digital PR strategy for SEO link building and backlink service clients. The value is not simply “getting links from a press release”; it is using press releases as a content asset that can generate brand mentions, referral traffic, indexed pages, and potential editorial backlinks when combined with proper outreach and SEO practices.

A possible strategy framework:

1. Use press releases to create linkable brand assets

Clients can publish announcements around genuinely newsworthy events, such as:

Product launches
Industry reports or research
Partnerships
Awards and certifications
New hires or leadership changes
Expansion into new markets
Case studies and milestones

The press release becomes a central asset that can be distributed through networks and referenced in outreach campaigns.

2. Combine press release distribution with backlink campaigns

For SEO clients, 1BillionLinks can be integrated as part of a multi-layer backlink strategy:

Phase 1 — Content creation

Write an SEO-optimised press release.
Include relevant branded anchors and natural references.
Link to important landing pages where appropriate.

Phase 2 — Distribution

Publish through press release channels and syndication networks.
Push the announcement to relevant industry publications, journalists, bloggers, and niche websites.

Phase 3 — Outreach amplification

Contact publications and influencers who cover the topic.
Pitch the story angle rather than just the link.
Encourage editorial coverage that can produce stronger contextual backlinks.

Phase 4 — SEO monitoring
Track:

New referring domains
Link quality
Brand mentions
Search visibility changes
Referral traffic
Indexed URLs
3. Position press releases as a “digital PR layer” rather than a standalone link tactic

Search engines increasingly evaluate links based on relevance, authority, and context. A press release service should be presented as supporting:

Brand authority
Entity recognition
Content discovery
Digital PR visibility
Natural link acquisition opportunities

The strongest outcomes usually come when press releases support other SEO activities, such as:

Guest posting
Expert commentary campaigns
Resource link building
HARO-style journalist outreach
Industry partnerships
Content marketing
4. Create client-specific press release campaigns

Examples:

SaaS company

Announcement:

“Company X launches AI workflow platform helping businesses reduce reporting time by 60%”

SEO objectives:

Build awareness around software keywords
Attract SaaS publications
Generate industry citations
Local business

Announcement:

“Company X opens new location and creates 25 jobs”

SEO objectives:

Improve local authority
Gain local media coverage
Support Google Business Profile visibility
E-commerce brand

Announcement:

“Brand X releases sustainable product line”

SEO objectives:

Gain lifestyle and sustainability coverage
Earn relevant product mentions
5. Use anchor text carefully

For backlink clients, avoid over-optimised anchor text patterns.

Better examples:

✅ Brand name links
“1BillionLinks”

✅ URL links
“example.com”

✅ Natural contextual links
“the company’s new SEO platform”

Avoid repeatedly building links like:

❌ “best SEO backlink service UK”
❌ “cheap link building packages”

A balanced link profile is more sustainable.

6. Build reporting around outcomes

Client reports can include:

Metric	Purpose
Press release views	Shows exposure
Indexed placements	Shows search visibility
Referring domains	Measures backlink growth
Domain authority metrics	Evaluates link quality
Brand mentions	Measures PR impact
Organic traffic changes	Shows SEO contribution
Example service positioning

A backlink agency could package 1BillionLinks as:

“A digital PR and authority-building channel that helps brands distribute newsworthy content, increase online visibility, and create opportunities for relevant backlinks through targeted press release campaigns.”

The key is to avoid selling press releases as a guaranteed ranking shortcut. They work best as part of a diversified SEO strategy where the goal is authority, relevance, and earned visibility rather than just accumulating links.
