**Why:** it is clear, safe, and on-trend. "AI Visibility" is the rising category term SEO buyers and
agencies already understand; "Baseline" sets honest expectations (a measured starting point + report),
which avoids any implication of guaranteed mentions/rankings. It reads as a credible, repeatable SEO
marketplace product rather than an experiment.
- **Order/CTA label:** "Start Your AI Visibility Baseline".
- **Strong alternative (campaign/short name):** "AI Mention Discovery" — benefit-oriented and ties to
  the internal project; safe because "discovery" = finding how you appear, not a guarantee. Use it as a
  marketing label if a punchier name is wanted; keep "AI Visibility Baseline" as the product name.
- Avoid as the primary name: "AI Answer Visibility" / "AI Mention Builder" (the latter implies we build
  mentions = overpromise).

## 2. What this service does
We test how AI tools currently understand your brand across a set of targeted questions, capture the
answers (with full-page screenshots), analyze which competitors and sources the AI associates with
your niche, and create crawlable support pages plus a clear report to guide your AI visibility
strategy. It's a baseline you can re-measure over time.

## 3. Why it matters
- AI assistants are becoming real discovery channels alongside search.
- You need to know **how (and whether) your brand shows up** in AI answers today.
- The **competitors** AI associates with your niche tell you who currently owns the space.
- Early **visibility gaps** are easy to identify from a baseline.
- **Crawlable support pages** give you structured, on-topic reference assets tied to your keywords.

## 10. FAQ
- **Will this make AI tools mention my brand?** Not necessarily — no service can guarantee AI mentions.
  We test and document how AI tools currently answer about your brand and give you a baseline plus
  supporting assets to guide your strategy.
- **Which AI tools do you test?** ChatGPT and Gemini (Starter); + Claude and Perplexity (Growth); +

- **Do you publish the pages for me?** We deliver the pages, a sitemap, and a publication/crawl
  checklist. Publishing on your domain or an SEOeStore support property is a setup step we coordinate;
  publishing helps discovery/crawling only — it doesn't guarantee indexing.
- **Do you provide screenshots?** Yes — full-page screenshots of each AI answer as proof.
- **Can agencies resell this?** Yes — deliverables are clean and white-label friendly.
- **How long does it take?** 3–10 business days depending on package (manual AI testing drives timing).
- **What do I receive at delivery?** A ZIP with screenshots, the master CSV tables, the crawlable
  support pages, the sitemap + checklist, and a PDF report.
