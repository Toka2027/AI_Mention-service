# Client-Safe Wording — Positioning & Disclaimers

Every client-facing artifact (report, pages, sales copy, emails) must follow this.

## Approved positioning (use these)
- AI visibility **baseline**
- LLM **query testing**
- AI **mention discovery**
- brand/entity **association review**
- crawlable **support pages**
- AI visibility **support assets**

One-line description:
> "AI Mention tests how AI tools currently answer questions about your brand, reviews your
> brand/entity associations and visibility gaps, and publishes crawlable support pages — delivered
> as a documented baseline report."

## Never say (banned)
- "injecting signals into LLMs" / "inject"
- "seeding signals" (as a claim of influence)
- "training the models" / "model training"
- "manipulating AI answers" / "forcing AI mentions"
- "influencing the AI" / "model influence"

## Never promise / guarantee
- guaranteed AI mentions
- guaranteed LLM visibility
- guaranteed indexing
- guaranteed search rankings
- model training or model influence

## Required framing
- The service is **observational**: it documents how AI tools currently respond at a point in time.
- Publishing support pages **helps discovery/crawling**; it does **not** guarantee indexing,
  rankings, or AI mentions.
- A brand not appearing today is a normal **baseline**, not a failure — it is the visibility gap
  the review surfaces, to be re-tested over time.

## Safe phrasings (swap table)
| Instead of | Use |
|---|---|
| "we'll get you mentioned in ChatGPT" | "we test and document how ChatGPT currently answers about your brand" |
| "guaranteed AI visibility" | "an AI visibility baseline you can re-measure over time" |
| "index your pages" | "publish crawlable support pages and submit a sitemap" |
| "improve your rankings" | "document AI answers and build supporting assets" |
